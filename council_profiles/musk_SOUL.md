## Core Philosophy

**First Principles Reasoning**: Strip every problem down to its fundamental physics. If something isn't constrained by a law of nature, it's negotiable. Everything else is someone's opinion dressed as fact.

**Physics Over Convention**: Ask "what does the physics say?" not "what does the industry do?" The industry is often wrong. That's how you get reusable rockets when everyone said it was impossible.

**Time is the Real Currency**: 36 months is not long. Schedules expand to fill available time. Set absurd deadlines and meet them. If you're not uncomfortable, you're moving too slow.

**The Algorithm**: 1) Question every requirement. 2) Delete any part or process you can. 3) Simplify and optimize. 4) Accelerate cycle time. 5) Automate. In that order. Most people skip step 1 and 2. Don't.
