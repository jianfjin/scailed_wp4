According to the SCAILED proposal, the partner **EPIDATA** has a key role in **Work Package 4 (WP4): Pathfinder System (Monitor and Evaluate Progress)**, jointly led with CHARITÉ. WP4 focuses on developing and validating the **“Pathfinder system”** — an interactive self-evaluation, monitoring, and navigation framework that helps stakeholders align with the EHDS strategic roadmap. 

## EPIDATA Main Responsibilities

### 1. Develop the Pathfinder System

EPIDATA is responsible for developing the first operational version of the Pathfinder system, including:

* self-evaluation tools,
* monitoring and evaluation guidelines,
* signposting/navigation tools,
* stakeholder-specific recommendation mechanisms.

The Pathfinder system is intended to help stakeholders:

* assess their current maturity,
* identify gaps,
* determine next technical, operational, and regulatory steps toward EHDS alignment. 

---

## Tasks Involving EPIDATA

### Task T4.2 — “Test Drive”

EPIDATA participates in validating the Pathfinder methodology with:

* Health Data Infrastructures,
* AI Factories,
* pilot use cases.

The validation includes collaboration with:

* LNDS (AI Factory),
* ELIXIR,
* cancer data infrastructures / national cancer nodes. 

This task supports:

* validation of real-world use cases,
* testing and validation of recommendations and guidelines,
* monitoring/evaluation alignment with pilot infrastructures and Secure Processing Environments (SPEs). 

---

### Task T4.3 — “Pathfinder System v2”

EPIDATA contributes to:

* revising the Pathfinder system based on practical implementation feedback,
* integrating feedback from WP2, WP3, WP5, WP6, WP7, and WP8,
* enriching the system with examples from validation activities,
* producing finalized monitoring and evaluation guidelines.

# Summary of EPIDATA Role

EPIDATA acts as one of the core operational and methodological partners in SCAILED by:

* designing the Pathfinder maturity/navigation framework,
* developing monitoring and evaluation methodologies,
* coordinating stakeholder feedback loops,
* validating the framework through pilots and AI Factories,
* delivering the first production-ready Pathfinder demo and associated guidelines.

Their role is central to transforming the project’s strategic roadmap into a practical operational guidance system for EHDS stakeholders.
