Yes. According to the proposal, EPIDATA’s work is highly interdependent with multiple other partners and work packages. The proposal explicitly emphasizes “cross-WP alignment and collaboration” to avoid siloed work. 

# 1. Are EPIDATA’s activities dependent on results from other partners?

Yes — heavily.

EPIDATA’s main role is in WP4 (“Pathfinder System”), whose purpose is to:

* transform strategic roadmap outputs into operational guidance,
* monitor and evaluate implementation progress,
* provide navigation/signposting tools for stakeholders.

To do this, EPIDATA requires inputs from many other WPs and partners.

---

## A. Dependency on WP3 — Strategic Roadmap & Landscape Analysis

WP4 explicitly states that it depends on:

* the Strategic Roadmap,
* landscape analysis,
* stakeholder analysis.

The proposal says:

> “With the input of the Strategic Roadmap that builds on the landscape analysis, this work package will develop self-evaluation tools…” 

This means EPIDATA depends on:

* SCIENSANO,
* BSC,
* other WP3 contributors,

to provide:

* roadmap structure,
* stakeholder pathways,
* governance models,
* AI deployment scenarios,
* EHDS integration recommendations.

Without WP3 outputs, EPIDATA cannot define:

* maturity models,
* monitoring criteria,
* navigation logic,
* evaluation indicators.

---

## B. Dependency on WP5 — EHDS Infrastructure Integration

WP4 also depends on pilot infrastructure implementations from WP5.

The proposal explicitly states:

* WP4 validates its methodology using WP5 pilots,
* Pathfinder is tested against “Pilot RIs and SPEs on AI Factories.” 

This means EPIDATA depends on:

* ELIXIR,
* LNDS,
* SCIENSANO,
* IISLAFE,
* CRG,
* AI Factories,
* HDAB participants.

They provide:

* real integration scenarios,
* governance constraints,
* interoperability implementations,
* EHDS operational feedback.

Without these pilots:

* Pathfinder cannot be validated,
* monitoring/evaluation logic cannot be tested realistically.

---

## C. Dependency on WP6 — Use Cases

WP4 depends strongly on WP6 real-world use cases.

The proposal says:

* WP6 provides “real-world validation data,”
* operational feedback,
* “lessons learnt.” 

Examples:

* federated learning,
* synthetic data,
* ICU AI diagnostics,
* polygenic risk scoring,
* AI clinical trial support.

EPIDATA needs these to:

* evaluate stakeholder journeys,
* test monitoring methods,
* refine guidance,
* build practical examples into Pathfinder V2. 

Without successful use cases:

* the Pathfinder system risks becoming theoretical only.

---

## D. Dependency on WP7 — AI Factories

WP4 validates Pathfinder against AI Factory implementations.

The proposal states:

* AI Factories are part of T4.2 validation,
* AI Factory integration informs Pathfinder iterations. 

EPIDATA depends on:

* BSC,
* LNDS,
* CESGA,
* USC,
* AI Antennas.

These provide:

* operational AI deployment models,
* secure compute workflows,
* EHDS-compliant processing environments.

---

## E. Dependency on WP2 — Stakeholder Forum

EPIDATA co-organizes stakeholder events and feedback collection:

* MS4.2,
* D4.2. 

Therefore EPIDATA depends on WP2 partners (especially IACS) for:

* stakeholder engagement,
* forum participation,
* external validation,
* policy alignment,
* ecosystem adoption.

# 2. Are other partners dependent on EPIDATA?

Yes — several WPs and partners depend on EPIDATA outputs.

This dependency is structural because the Pathfinder system becomes:

* the operational guidance layer,
* the monitoring framework,
* the navigation system for the entire project.

---

## A. WP5 Depends on EPIDATA

WP5 pilots are evaluated using the Pathfinder framework.

The proposal says:

* Pathfinder validates pilot infrastructures,
* provides monitoring/evaluation guidance,
* identifies next implementation steps. 

This means:

* ELIXIR,
* LNDS,
* HDABs,
* infrastructure operators

depend on EPIDATA for:

* assessment methodology,
* maturity evaluation,
* operational guidance.

---

## B. WP6 Depends on EPIDATA

Use case teams depend on Pathfinder outputs to:

* assess readiness,
* identify deployment gaps,
* align with EHDS requirements,
* navigate regulatory pathways.

WP6 generates lessons learned,
but EPIDATA converts them into reusable frameworks and guidelines.

---

## C. WP7 Depends on EPIDATA

AI Factories depend on Pathfinder to:

* assess EHDS alignment,
* evaluate governance maturity,
* structure operational compliance.

EPIDATA essentially creates:

* the “how mature are we?”
* and “what should we do next?”
  framework.

---

## D. WP2 / Stakeholder Ecosystem Depends on EPIDATA

The proposal explicitly says Pathfinder becomes:

* a “navigation system,”

Thus:

* stakeholders,
* biotech companies,
* HDABs,

depend on EPIDATA outputs to operationalize project recommendations.

---

# 3. Overall dependency pattern

The architecture is highly circular and iterative:

| Dependency Direction | Nature                                                                                |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Other WPs → EPIDATA  | Inputs, pilots, validation data, roadmap, real implementations                        |
| EPIDATA → Other WPs  | Monitoring framework, navigation system, evaluation methodology, operational guidance |

The proposal explicitly describes this as:

* “Analyse → Implement → Test → Scale”
  with synchronized iteration and feedback loops across WPs. 

---

# 4. Strategic interpretation

EPIDATA is not positioned as:

* a standalone technical implementer,
* nor as an isolated software vendor.

Instead, EPIDATA acts as:

* an orchestration,
* methodology,
* operationalization,
* evaluation,
* and navigation layer across the entire consortium.

This means:

* EPIDATA cannot fully execute without upstream technical and policy inputs,
* but the consortium also cannot operationalize and scale outputs effectively without EPIDATA’s Pathfinder framework.
