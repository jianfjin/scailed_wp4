Consensus

9/9 seats agree

Unanimous Verdict

  • 本质：Pathfinder = 带约束的图搜索 (Dijkstra/A*) + 极简问卷界面。不是 Web App，不是平台。
  • 架构：Monolith FastAPI + PostgreSQL。No K8s, no 微服务, no Neo4j, no GraphQL。Docker Compose 单容器。
  • 规则引擎：声明式 YAML 外部化，热加载。非程序员（Epidata QA 团队）可审阅。
  • 生死线：M3 前 WP2/3/8 Schema 签字。无签字 = 拒绝开工。
  • 交付物：Python 库 pathfinder.solve() + FastAPI wrapper + Vue 3 前端。内核 > 外壳。
  • LLM 使用：仅用于自然语言生成和文档解析。路径选择必须确定性、可审计。
  • 合同：预付款30% + 里程碑绑定上游 + V2 独立合同 + 返工按人天另计。

Risk Register

5 critical risks

🚨 WP3 delivers PDF instead of structured JSON

If CHARITE provides roadmap as a PDF with colored arrows, the entire graph engine is blocked. Mitigation: require JSON/CSV schema sign-off by M2, with topological sort proof.

Probability: Very High · Impact: Critical · Owner: Feng Ge

🚨 WP8 legal constraints are natural language, not executable rules

Lawyers write "should" and "may" — SAT solvers need boolean predicates. Mitigation: soft checks + YAML hot-loading; flag risks rather than hard-block.

Probability: High · Impact: High · Owner: Dijkstra + Guido

⚠️ Ambiguous requirements cause endless rework

Epidata says "build the tool" but can't define what it is. Mitigation: M1 low-fi prototype with 3 options, signed scope boundary.

Probability: Very High · Impact: High · Owner: Feng Ge

⚠️ D4.1 acceptance criteria are subjective

"Report + demo" means Lu Zhao's subjective judgment. Mitigation: quantified acceptance criteria in contract.

Probability: Medium-High · Impact: Medium · Owner: Legal

⚠️ EHDS implementing acts change mid-project

Rules written in M1 based on drafts may be obsolete by M30. Mitigation: hot-loadable rule engine. Never hard-code compliance.

Probability: Medium-High · Impact: Medium · Owner: Guido
